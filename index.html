A New Chapter in a Journey of Service

A Lifetime of Leadership, Service & Community

I am standing as an independent candidate for the Victorian Legislative Council, seeking to represent the people of the Western Metropolitan Region — motivated by a lifelong commitment to service, integrity, fairness, inclusion and practical leadership.

My guiding philosophy is rooted in Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam — “the world is one family” — and in these timeless principles:

Sarva Jan Hitaya — the wellbeing of all
Sarve Bhavantu Sukhinah — may all be happy
Sarve Santu Niramayah — may all be free from illness and suffering
